FRC 2023 Robot

A FIRST Robotics Competition robot pairing the shifting swerve drivetrain with a 3-jointed arm whose joints are all driven from the base - keeping the arm's moving mass low and the robot's center of gravity down.

Skills: Fusion 360 CAD · CNC Machining · SLS / FDM 3D Printing · Mechanism Design

Back to all projects

Key Mechanisms

  • Shifting swerve drivetrain - the 2-speed swerve modules, shifting between a high-torque low gear and a fast cycling gear.
  • 3-jointed arm, base-driven - all three joints are driven from the base of the arm rather than by motors mounted at each joint. Keeping the motors off the arm reduces its moving mass and inertia and keeps the robot's center of gravity low.

Design & Manufacturing

Like the 2024 robot, this machine was designed in Fusion 360 and manufactured in-house: CNC mill and CNC lathe for precision components, water jet and fiber laser cutter for plate work, and SLS and FDM 3D printing for complex and quick-turn parts.
